This policy explains what information {{ b.name }} collects through {{ b.site }}, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and gender (used to show suitable salons), and a password that we store only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose it, we receive your name and verified email address from Google. We do not get your Google password.
  • Bookings: the salon, service, time, status and visit code of your bookings, and reviews you write.
  • Location: only when you allow your browser to share it, to show salons near you. Salon owners can save their salon's location.
  • Payments: processed by Razorpay. We receive the payment status, amount and reference numbers, never your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Technical data: basic log information such as IP address and browser type, used for security and to keep the site running.

How we use it

  • To create your account, show salons and slots, and handle bookings and plans.
  • To send one-time codes and booking messages by email (and in-site notifications).
  • To prevent misuse, fix problems and improve the service.
  • To meet legal requirements.

Who we share it with

  • The salon you book: your name, phone number and booking details, so they can serve you.
  • Service providers that help us run the site: hosting (Vercel), database (Neon), email delivery, Google (sign-in) and Razorpay (payments). They may only use the data to provide their service.
  • Authorities when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in and to protect forms. We may use analytics tools to understand how the site is used.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed for bookings, payments records and legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or to close your account, by writing to {{ b.email }}. We will reply within a reasonable time. You can turn off location sharing in your browser at any time.

Security

We use encrypted connections, hashed passwords and access controls. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.
