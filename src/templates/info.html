About Trimzee

Trimzee is a salon booking website that helps customers find nearby salons, check available times, and book a visit. Salon owners can list their services and manage bookings.

Our aim is to make finding and booking a salon straightforward for customers and salon teams.

For a question about a salon service or a payment made directly to a salon, please contact that salon as well.

Refund Policy

Trimzee does not currently collect online payments for customer salon bookings. Customers pay the salon directly for services, so Trimzee has no booking payment to refund.

For a refund or adjustment relating to a service paid for at a salon, contact that salon directly. If you believe Trimzee has charged you in error, email trimzee.in01@gmail.com with the details.

If online customer payments are introduced, this policy will be updated before those payments are accepted.

Privacy Policy

This page describes information Trimzee processes to provide its salon discovery and booking features. The details below reflect the website's current features and may change as the service changes.

Information we process

  • Account details such as your name, email address, account role, gender preference, and phone number. Passwords are stored as password hashes.
  • Booking details such as the salon, service, appointment time, booking status, confirmation code, and contact phone number.
  • Salon details supplied by salon owners, including business name, services, prices, contact details, and salon location.
  • Reviews, referral activity, and messages you send to the Trimzee assistant.
  • Technical information such as your network address may be processed for abuse prevention and service security.

Location

If you allow location access, your browser provides your location to help find nearby salons. When you confirm a booking, the location is sent to Trimzee to check that the salon is within the booking distance. The booking flow uses it for that check; it does not save your coordinates as booking details.

How information is used and shared

We use information to operate accounts, show salons and available times, manage bookings, send service-related notices, prevent abuse, and provide customer support. Booking details are available to the relevant salon so it can handle your appointment.

Depending on which integrations are configured, information may be processed by Google when you choose Google sign-in, an email provider for verification or account messages, Twilio for booking messages sent through WhatsApp, and Anthropic when the AI assistant is enabled. Links to services such as Google Maps or WhatsApp open those services under their own privacy terms.

Storage and your choices

Account and booking information is stored to operate the service. We do not currently publish a fixed retention period. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, contact trimzee.in01@gmail.com. Some information may need to be retained to complete bookings, protect the service, or meet legal obligations.

Location access can be denied or withdrawn in your browser settings. Without it, location-dependent booking features may not work.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email trimzee.in01@gmail.com.

This notice reflects the current application code. Please contact us if you need clarification.
