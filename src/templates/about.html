About Trimzee

Our service

Trimzee is an online booking platform that helps customers review salon services, prices, and appointment availability. Customers can send a booking request, see its status, and use a visit code when they arrive.

Local businesses provide their own service information, prices, hours, and appointment decisions. A request is not confirmed until the business accepts it. Unless online payment is offered at checkout, customers pay the salon directly for its services.

Location access is optional while browsing. It is requested when a customer chooses the location feature and is needed to check the booking area before a request is placed.
