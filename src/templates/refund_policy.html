Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

Booking a salon

  • Sending a booking request is free. You can cancel from My bookings any time before your appointment.
  • Unless online payment is offered at checkout, you pay the salon directly, so there is nothing to refund. If online payment for bookings is offered, the cancellation and refund terms shown at checkout apply to that payment.

Plans (Salon plan and Trimzee Plus)

  • Plans are digital services and are activated right after the payment is confirmed. They are bought for a fixed period and do not renew automatically.
  • Because the service is available to you from the moment it is activated, plan payments are not refundable, except in these cases:
    • you were charged twice for the same plan, or charged but the plan was not activated;
    • a technical fault on our side stopped you from using the plan for a significant time;
    • a refund is required by law.
  • To ask for a refund, write to {{ b.email }} within 7 days of the payment with your registered email address and the payment reference.

Refund timeline

  • Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5-7 working days. Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show it.
  • If a payment fails or is deducted without the plan being activated, the amount is returned automatically by the payment provider or bank, usually within 5-7 working days. If not, contact us.

Coupons

Coupon codes have no cash value and cannot be refunded or exchanged. A discount used at checkout reduces the amount paid; any refund is for the amount actually paid.

Delivery of the service

Plans are delivered online, instantly. If your plan is not active within 24 hours of a successful payment, contact us and we will fix it or refund you.
