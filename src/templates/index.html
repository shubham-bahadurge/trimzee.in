A simpler way to plan your visit

Trimzee brings service details, starting prices, and appointment information together so you can choose a local salon with confidence. Search by business or service, compare the options shown, and open a listing to review its services, hours, and available times.

Choose a service and time

On a listing, select one or more services and choose an open time that suits you. Add your phone number and send the request. The salon must accept it before the appointment is confirmed; you can check its status later in My bookings.

Know what to expect

Each request includes a visit code. Show it when you arrive so the owner can complete the booking. Unless online payment is offered at checkout, payment for the service is made directly to the salon. Prices, hours, and service details are supplied by each business, so contact the owner if you need to confirm something before visiting.

Use location only when you want to

Browsing does not require location access. Tap the pin beside search to share your device location and sort participating businesses by distance. If you skip that step, you can still explore the listings and open their details. Location access is also needed to place a booking, so the app can check that the selected business is within the booking area.
